The Story

I am Zeyad El-Sayed Ahmed, a self-taught music man. I turn records into results — A&R, production, distribution and the campaign around it.

Over the last five years I have moved between the studio and the spreadsheet — signing 60+ artists, brokering cross-border deals across Egypt, Morocco and Ukraine, and pioneering the first Dolby Atmos rollout for Arab artists. I understand the music before the business, and that is exactly why the business works.

  • Directed marketing & PR for Lege-Cy – Placebo (2023): repositioned him from underground to commercial mainstream and curated the Sass Club album launch as a press event.
  • Signed 30+ publishing deals (mechanical, sync, performance) — copyright transactions from initial terms through executed agreements.
  • Coordinated CBC Music Hub media appearances for DJ Totti and Nubi — full logistics, scheduling and artist communications.
  • Managed the digital release of the Lel Egar film trailer (Khaled El Sawy, Mohamed Salam) — later a Luxor Film Festival winner, now streaming on Netflix Egypt.
  • Scaled the signed roster to 60+ artists across Egyptian pop, North African rap and Gulf markets.
